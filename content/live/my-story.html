No life surrendered to Him is ever wasted

Sylvia Suhr writes faith-based books that help readers overcome anxiety, fear and uncertainty by learning to trust God more deeply.

Born into poverty in post-war England, Sylvia’s early years were shaped by hardship, but also by hope. She came to know Jesus personally at thirty-eight, and at fifty-seven answered God’s call to ministry.

For more than twenty years she travelled in Africa and Asia, walking alongside the poor, the broken and the forgotten. She ministered in Kenya, Nigeria, the Philippines and the United Kingdom, and helped establish Heaven on Earth Ministries for orphans and vulnerable children.

About Sylvia Suhr

One Life Led by God

Sylvia Suhr is a Christian author, speaker, and missionary based in Australia. She writes faith-based books that help readers overcome anxiety, fear, and uncertainty by learning to trust God more deeply.

Her work focuses on Christian living, spiritual growth, and finding lasting peace through Christ. Through inspirational Christian books and devotionals on anxiety, trust, and spiritual growth, Sylvia gently guides readers toward freedom, hope, and lasting peace in Christ.

Having travelled extensively through Africa and Asia in missionary work, Sylvia carries a deep passion for helping people discover their true identity in Christ and live with purpose and courage.

Her books, including From Fear to Freedom and One Life, The Narrow Path, are written from lived experience and a lifelong walk with God.

Sylvia continues to write, teach, and encourage others through her ministry and publishing work at Sylvia’s Dream.

My name is Sylvia Suhr, and I’ve come to know firsthand that when you place your life in God’s hands, He will do far more than you ever imagined.

In Sylvia’s words

My story

1

From Australia to Africa

My story began in Australia, where I raised my family while also discovering a deeper relationship with Jesus. He became not just a Savior to me, but my companion, provider, and guide. Eventually, the Lord began stirring my heart for missions. It was a call I could not ignore.

2

A Mission to the Nations

That calling took me across the world, to remote villages in Malawi, the streets of Kenya, and the vibrant communities of the Philippines. I saw lives changed, hearts healed, and hope restored as we ministered God’s love in practical ways — through teaching, counseling, prayer, and simply showing up.

3

One Life Can Make a Difference

I’ve come to believe this deeply: one surrendered life can touch nations. It’s not about being perfect — it’s about being willing. Whether you’re walking through hardship, waiting on breakthrough, or wondering if your life really matters — know this: God has a purpose for your journey.

4

Why I Write

The books and devotionals I share are extensions of this life — testaments to the faithfulness of God. My desire is that through my words, others will discover their worth, embrace their identity in Christ, and trust Him in every season.
