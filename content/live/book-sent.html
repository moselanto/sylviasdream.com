Preparing Your Heart for Missions

By Sylvia Suhr

Many people feel a stirring toward missions. They long to serve God in distant places and share the message of Christ with those who have never heard. But before God sends someone across nations, He prepares the heart.

In SENT: Preparing Your Heart for Missions, Sylvia Suhr shares lessons learned through many years of walking with God and participating in mission journeys around the world. This book explores the spiritual foundations that shape a life ready to serve.

What the Book Covers

This book explores:

  • The empowering work of the Holy Spirit
  • The strength found in praying in tongues
  • Hearing God’s voice clearly
  • The importance of spiritual guidance and confirmation
  • Living a disciplined life of prayer and fellowship
