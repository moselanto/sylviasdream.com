ONE LIFE: The Narrow Path by Sylvia Suhr

This book is more than a story.

It is a journey of faith, surrender, purpose, and discovering what truly matters in life.

Over many years, I travelled through remote regions of Africa and Asia, walking alongside the poor, the broken, and the forgotten. Through heartbreak, miracles, and unexpected moments of grace, I discovered something life-changing:

“Comfortable is scary.”

This newly refreshed hardcover edition carries a message of hope, courage, compassion, and walking the narrow path with God.

If you have ever longed to:

  • live with deeper purpose
  • trust God more fully
  • step beyond fear and comfort
  • make a difference with your life

…then this book is for you.

Your life has purpose. Your story matters. And one surrendered life can impact many others.
