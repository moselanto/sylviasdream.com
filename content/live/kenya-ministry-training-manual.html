Kenya Ministry Training Manual

This comprehensive Training Manual for Short Term Missions to Kenya is your essential companion for making your mission trip both impactful and God-honouring. Whether you're setting out for the first time or returning to serve again, this guide is filled with biblical insights, cultural understanding, and practical tools to help you walk in love, humility, and spiritual authority.

Written with firsthand experience and a heart for the people of Kenya, this manual equips you with:

  • Biblical foundations for evangelism and healing ministry
  • Cultural tips for respectful and effective cross-cultural engagement
  • Guidelines for prayer, preaching, team conduct, and local partnerships
  • Practical steps for personal preparation and team unity

Be empowered to fulfill the Great Commission with grace, wisdom, and joy. Step into the field as an ambassador of Christ, ready to sow seeds of faith, compassion, and transformation.
