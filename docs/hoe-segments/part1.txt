<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Supporting Orphans and Education in Rural Kenya</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className": "sd-lead"} -->
<p class="sd-lead">Heaven on Earth is a registered Christian charity and church based in Kenya, dedicated to supporting orphans, vulnerable children, and families living in poverty. Through faith-based education, church ministry, and sustainable community development programs, we work to bring long-term transformation to rural communities in Kenya.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our mission focuses on rescuing vulnerable children, providing education, building schools, and empowering families through income-generating projects. We believe lasting change happens when communities are strengthened through Christian faith, education, and opportunity.</p>
