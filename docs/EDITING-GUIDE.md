# Editing your website: a plain-English guide for Sylvia

Your website is built so you can change any page yourself, like a page builder, without touching code. Everything happens in **WordPress Admin** (yoursite/wp-admin).

## 1. Change text or a photo on a page
1. Go to **Pages**, then click the page you want (for example **Home**).
2. Click any text and type. It works like a word processor.
3. To change a photo, click it, then choose **Replace** in the small toolbar above it, and pick a photo from your Media Library or upload a new one.
4. Click **Update** (top right) to save. Nothing changes on the live site until you click Update.

Tip: **Ctrl+Z** (Windows) or **Cmd+Z** (Mac) undoes your last change.

## 2. Add a ready-made section
1. Click the **+** button (top left) and open the **Patterns** tab.
2. Choose **Sylvia's Dream**. You will see sections such as Hero, Featured books, About Sylvia band, Heaven on Earth band, Dream Partners band, Testimonials, Newsletter and contact, Page header, FAQ, Two columns: text and image, Call to action strip and Contact layout.
3. Click a section to add it, then change its words and photos.

## 3. Move or remove a section
- Click inside the section, then use the **up and down arrows** in the toolbar to move it, or drag the handle (six dots).
- To remove it, click the **three dots** in the toolbar and choose **Delete**.
- The **List View** button (three lines, top left) shows every section on the page so you can pick one easily.

## 4. Change a button
Click the button, type the new words, and click the **link** icon to change where it goes.

## 5. Add or change a book
1. Go to **Products**, then **Add New** (or click an existing book).
2. Enter the title, description, price, and cover (**Product image** on the right).
3. For ebooks, tick **Virtual** and **Downloadable** and upload the file.
4. Click **Publish**. The book appears automatically on the Books page and in the Featured books section.

## 6. Change the menu
Go to **Appearance > Menus**. Drag items to reorder them, or drag an item slightly to the right to put it inside a dropdown (for example Devotionals under Books). Click **Save Menu**.

## 6a. Menu details
- Turn on **Screen Options > Description** in Appearance > Menus to edit the small line under each dropdown item (for example "Author, speaker and missionary").
- The Books menu is a wide panel. Its column headings are links with "#" as the address; drag books links under the right heading.
- On phones, a bar at the bottom shows Home, Books, Ministry, Give and More. Change it in Appearance > Customize > Sylvia's Dream > Header and menu.

## 7. Change the footer, Give button, or social links
Go to **Appearance > Customize > Sylvia's Dream**. Each part of the header and footer has a box you can type into. Click **Publish** to save.

## 8. Page layouts
On the right of any page, under **Template**:
- **Default**: page title banner and a comfortable reading width.
- **Full width (page builder)**: you design the whole page with sections.
- **Canvas (no header or footer)**: a blank page, for special landing pages.

## Please do not change
- **Plugins** and **Themes** (ask Moses first).
- **WooCommerce > Settings > Payments**, or anything in Stripe.
- The download and thank-you pages for past buyers.
- **Settings > Sylvia's Dream** redirects.

If something looks wrong, open the page, click the clock icon (**Revisions**) to go back to an earlier version, or contact Moses.
