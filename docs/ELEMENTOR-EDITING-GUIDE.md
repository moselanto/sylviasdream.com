# How to change your website

This guide shows you how to change the words and pictures on your website. You cannot break anything: every change can be undone.

## Before you start

1. Open your website's control panel. Your web designer will give you the address and your password.
2. Sign in with your name and password.
3. You now see the Dashboard. At the top there is a box called **How to edit your website**. It has the same steps as this guide.

## Change the words or a picture on a page

1. On the left, click **Pages**. You see a list of your pages.
2. Move your mouse over the page you want to change, for example **Home**.
3. Click **Edit with Elementor**. Your page opens, with a panel on the left.
4. Click on the part of the page you want to change. The panel on the left now shows that part.
5. In the panel you see four headings. Click one to open it:
   - **Texts**: the words. Click in a box and type.
   - **Photos**: the pictures. Click the picture, then **Media Library** to choose another one, or **Upload files** to add a new photo from your computer.
   - **Links and buttons**: the words on a button and which page it opens.
   - **Settings**: small choices, for example how many books to show.
6. You see your change on the page straight away.
7. When you are happy, click the green **Update** button at the bottom of the panel. Your change is now on the website.

## Made a mistake?

- Press **Ctrl** and **Z** together (on a Mac: **Cmd** and **Z**) to undo the last change.
- Or click the small clock at the bottom of the panel (**History**). Click an earlier moment to go back to it.
- If you closed the page without clicking **Update**, nothing was changed.

## Add a new part to a page

1. Click the small **grid** button at the top of the panel. You now see all the parts you can add.
2. At the top is the group **Sylvia's Dream**. These parts look exactly like the rest of your website. For example:
   - **Meet Sylvia**
   - **Featured books**
   - **Latest devotionals**
   - **Newsletter sign-up**
   - **Dream Partners (giving)**
   - **Button**
3. Hold the mouse button down on a part, move it onto the page where you want it, and let go.
4. Change its words in **Texts** and click **Update**.

To remove a part: right-click it on the page and choose **Delete**.

## Things you change somewhere else

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Books, prices and book covers | **Products** on the left. Click the book's name. |
| A new devotional | **Posts**, then **Add New**. |
| The menu at the top | **Appearance**, then **Menus**. |
| Facebook, Instagram and your email address | **Appearance**, then **Sylvia's Dream Theme Options**, then **Contact and social links**. |
| The number of Dream Partners | **Settings**, then **Sylvia's Dream**. |

## Please do not change

- The **Buy ebook** buttons and the giving buttons on the Give page. They are connected to your payments, so they always keep working. That is why you cannot change where they go.
- The contact form fields. Ask your web designer if you would like a change.

## Good habits

- Use only photos you are allowed to use.
- Under each new photo, type a short description in **Describe the photo**. It helps people who cannot see.
- Keep one main title at the top of each page.
- If anything looks wrong, close the page without clicking **Update** and ask your web designer.
