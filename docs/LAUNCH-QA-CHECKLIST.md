# Sylvia's Dream — Launch QA Checklist

## Content approval
- [ ] Sylvia approves biography, portrait, titles, book descriptions and quotations.
- [ ] Every book cover, edition, format, price, availability and retailer URL is verified.
- [ ] Ministry name, registration/legal details, programmes, geography and any financial/impact claims are verified.
- [ ] Testimonials, photos and videos have appropriate permission and attribution.
- [ ] Free resources are approved for distribution.
- [ ] Privacy, terms and refunds are reviewed against actual services and legal obligations.

## Navigation and responsive UI
- [ ] Every header, footer, card, CTA, breadcrumb and dropdown route works.
- [ ] Mobile dock routes work and active state follows the current page.
- [ ] Menu opens/closes by keyboard, Escape and touch; focus remains visible.
- [ ] Test at 320px, 375px, 768px, 1024px and 1440px.
- [ ] Mobile dock and back-to-top control do not cover forms or content.
- [ ] Reduced-motion, zoom, contrast and accessible labels are checked.

## Functional integration
- [ ] Contact form has server-side validation, anti-spam and verified delivery.
- [ ] Newsletter consent, provider integration, confirmation and unsubscribe work.
- [ ] Payment-provider test mode passes success, failure and duplicate event tests.
- [ ] Donation amount, frequency, recipient, currency, receipts and cancellation work.
- [ ] Checkout totals and order confirmation match the verified order state.
- [ ] Download links are private, time-limited and order-authorised.
- [ ] Retailer and social URLs are real, official and tested.
- [ ] Videos and audio use approved URLs and are usable on mobile.

## SEO, security and performance
- [ ] Unique titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, sitemap and redirects are verified.
- [ ] Structured data is accurate and passes validation.
- [ ] Images are responsive, compressed, dimensioned and correctly described.
- [ ] No critical console errors; no broken internal links or mixed-content warnings.
- [ ] Backups and rollback plan exist; staging is not accidentally indexed.
- [ ] Core Web Vitals are checked on representative devices/networks.

## Launch approval
- [ ] Sylvia approves desktop and mobile pages in writing.
- [ ] All service owners sign off integrations and monitoring.
- [ ] Production deployment and rollback window are agreed.
- [ ] Post-launch checks scheduled for forms, checkout, donations, downloads, analytics and indexing.
