How I Can Help You

You don’t have to navigate the publishing journey alone.

Whether you are still shaping your manuscript, preparing to publish, or wondering how to promote your finished book, I can walk alongside you with practical, personal guidance.

Book Clarity Session

For writers who have an idea, an unfinished manuscript, or simply feel unsure about the next step.

Together we can look at your message, audience, structure, title, direction, and what needs to happen next.

Ideal for: first-time authors, stalled manuscripts, or anyone needing clarity and encouragement.

Manuscript to Market

For authors who want support taking a completed or nearly completed manuscript through the publishing process.

This may include manuscript review, structure, editing guidance, formatting preparation, cover and back-cover wording, book descriptions, publishing platforms, pricing, keywords, categories, and launch preparation.

Ideal for: authors who want someone beside them from manuscript preparation through to publication.

Book Launch & Marketing Support

Publishing the book is only the beginning. I can help you prepare to introduce your book confidently to readers.

Support may include launch planning, social-media posts, promotional graphics, QR codes, author pages, book descriptions, website copy, email promotion, and ideas for reaching the right readers.

Ideal for: newly published authors or authors wanting to give an existing book fresh visibility.
