Christian Book on Anxiety and Trust in God

Step Out of Fear. Step Into Peace.

From Fear to Freedom is a warm and powerful invitation to move beyond anxiety and into the steady calm that faith provides.

Drawing from personal experience, Scripture, and lived encounters with God’s faithfulness, Sylvia Suhr walks alongside those who feel burdened by worry or weighed down by uncertainty. This book does not promise a life without challenges—but it reveals how trust in God brings clarity, strength, and hope in every season.

From Fear to Freedom is a Christian book for anyone struggling with anxiety, fear, or uncertainty about the future. Rooted in Scripture and personal experience, this faith-based guide helps readers grow in trust, peace, and confidence in Christ.

With honesty and grace, each chapter explores:

  • The roots of fear
  • The lies that fuel anxiety
  • The quiet power of surrender
  • The freedom found in resting in God’s presence.
