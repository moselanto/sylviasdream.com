Supporting Orphans and Education in Rural Kenya

Heaven on Earth is a registered Christian charity and church based in Kenya, dedicated to supporting orphans, vulnerable children, and families living in poverty. Through faith-based education, church ministry, and sustainable community development programs, we work to bring long-term transformation to rural communities in Kenya.

Our mission focuses on rescuing vulnerable children, providing education, building schools, and empowering families through income-generating projects. We believe lasting change happens when communities are strengthened through Christian faith, education, and opportunity.

Building Heaven on Earth

More than a mission — it’s a movement

Heaven on Earth Ministries is more than a mission — it’s a movement of mercy, born from love and lived in the dust and beauty of Kenya.

I’ve had the privilege of helping establish this ministry and living among the people for many years, sharing in their joys, struggles, and breakthroughs. We care for orphans and vulnerable children, offering not only food and education, but dignity and hope.

Every gift matters. Around 90% of donations go directly to the work in Kenya — feeding programs, medical care, school fees, tuition, uniforms, books, and stationery. The remaining 10% supports essential administration.

Today, many of our children are thriving in universities, studying Civil Engineering, IT, Business Management, Nursing, and more. We believe each child is called to rise with integrity, discipline, and faith — to become a light in their generation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this monthly or one-time?

Dream Partners give monthly. If you prefer a one-time donation, you are welcome to do that too.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your monthly support at any time.

Do I receive a receipt?

Yes, your payment provider will issue a receipt for your donation.

How do I know my donation is used well?

We provide regular updates and work closely with trusted local leaders on the ground in Kenya to ensure transparency and accountability.

I don’t live in Australia — can I still give?

A Dream Partner can support from anywhere in the world.

You will receive updates

Dream Partners receive regular updates and stories so you can see how your support is making a real difference in rural Kenya.
