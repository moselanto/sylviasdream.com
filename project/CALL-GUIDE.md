# Call with Sylvia: agenda and decisions to capture

45 minutes. Melbourne is 8 hours ahead of Kenya. Google Meet is better for screen sharing. The full prep guide and spoken script are in Pimofy's Town library.

## Agenda
1. Welcome (3 min). Follow her lead if she opens in prayer.
2. Recap of what is agreed (5 min): see `PROJECT-BRIEF.md`.
3. Prototype walkthrough (10 min): Home, a book page, Heaven on Earth and Give, Author Support, "For Sylvia's review", then on a phone. New features are noted for a change order, not agreed on the call.
4. Book list questions (10 min): `BOOK-CATALOGUE.md`.
5. Access and backups (8 min): WordPress invite, the minimum Stripe items (`STRIPE-DATA-REQUEST.md`), private Drive folder, domain registrar, honest note on restore time.
6. Payments and giving (4 min): how Dream Partners give today (payment links or WordPress.com button, which carries 8%), PayPal Business, old payment links decided later in writing.
7. Timeline, deposit and close (5 min).

- [ ] Domain registrar and renewal date

Asking for passwords; promising a fee is zero; agreeing new features; touching Stripe; changing the live site before backups; running long.
