## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- Dear Sylvia,
- Thank you for reaching out, and for your kind words about the Cistern of Hope Foundation website. It is a pleasure to connect with you and to learn about your books, your ministry and the work of Heaven on Earth in Kenya.
- As you requested, we reviewed sylviasdream.com page by page: 55 pages and 14 blog posts. The site has a strong foundation, with more than 20 books, a regular Grace for the Journey blog, Stripe payments and a growing subscriber list. However, it has grown one page at a time and now works against you in a few important ways: visitors meet a menu of almost 30 links, there are three different home pages and several duplicate book pages, paid ebook download pages are listed publicly for search engines, most images have no descriptions, and there are no privacy or refund policies. Our full findings are set out in the accompanying Website Review & Recommendations document.
- This proposal sets out a focused redesign on your existing WordPress.com site, so you keep your domain, content, subscribers and Stripe account. You get a modern author and ministry website, one proper book shop with secure ebook delivery, a clear Dream Partner giving page for Heaven on Earth, and an SEO clean-up so more readers can find your books.
- Everything is delivered for a fixed price of USD 280, including set-up of your full catalogue of current titles, within 10 to 12 working days depending on how quickly we review and agree on revisions together. The website is delivered on your current WordPress.com Personal plan, so no plan upgrade or migration is required. Third-party charges, such as Stripe and PayPal fees, are paid directly by you.

## SCOPE OF WORK


### A. Discovery & Content Audit

- Kick-off video call to confirm goals, audiences (readers, ministry supporters, authors) and priorities
- Inventory of all 69 pages and posts, with a decision for each: keep, merge, redirect or retire
- Confirmed site map and book catalogue

### B. Website Redesign (WordPress.com)

- Replace the 2019 default Twenty Twenty theme with a custom-designed author theme built for the Sylvia's Dream brand (existing logo retained and refined for web)
- Design keeps the warm gold and cream style you liked in the prototype, with large, easy-to-read text, especially on phones, and simple navigation
- Your location is shown as Australia only; no street address or town appears on the site
- Mobile-first homepage and templates for book pages, blog, ministry and resource pages
- Simplified main menu of 6 to 7 items with dropdowns, plus a full footer with contact, social, newsletter and policy links
- One home page, with the extra /home/ and /new-home/ pages retired and redirected
- Tested on phones, tablets and desktops in Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge

### C. Content Consolidation & Copy Editing

- Merge duplicate book pages (for example three From Fear to Freedom and three The Hidden Key pages) into one page per book, with 301 redirects
- Merge My Story and My Autobiography into one About Sylvia page
- Your recently updated Author Support wording is kept exactly as you wrote it
- Final biography, Heaven on Earth and ministry information published only after your review for accuracy
- Wording about book purchases supporting Heaven on Earth published only after your approval
- Proofreading and consistent formatting of headings, buttons and prices, including the Fast Foreword menu typo and stray Categories labels
- Update outdated content such as the Coming Soon notice on Manifesting Miracles Now

### D. Book Shop & Secure Digital Delivery

- One shop with cart and checkout (WooCommerce on WordPress.com), replacing about 10 separate Stripe payment links
- Card payments through your existing Stripe account, plus PayPal; prices in AUD
- Secure, expiring download links emailed to buyers, with downloads removed from public pages
- No book or resource, including the book-writing guide, is listed as a free download without your approval
- Amazon, Kobo, Barnes & Noble and Spotify links cleaned up and shown consistently on each book
- Set-up of your complete current catalogue (25 titles on the book confirmation list, including Fields of Becoming, When the River Flows Again, Guard Your Heart Renew Your Mind and Angelique Finds Her Light), using the titles, descriptions, formats and prices you confirm
- Existing buyers keep access: old download links stay working (hidden from search) during a transition period, and past buyers are emailed a new secure link before old files are retired

### E. Dream Partner Giving & Heaven on Earth

- Redesigned Heaven on Earth page with registration details, programmes, where giving goes, gallery and updates
- Existing monthly Stripe giving subscriptions and payment links are left untouched and continue without interruption; new Dream Partners join through the same Stripe account
- Monthly giving levels of $5, $15, $30 and $50, with the option to choose another amount, plus one-off gifts and the GoFundMe campaign
- Automatic thank-you emails and receipts, and a progress bar toward the goal of 100 Dream Partners

### F. Email List & Lead Magnets

- Email sign-up flow that delivers a welcome resource automatically, using only a resource you have approved
- Sign-up forms on the home page, book pages and blog posts
- Sign-up connected to your existing WordPress.com subscriber list

### G. Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)

- Clear page titles and meta descriptions for every page (the current home page title runs to about 150 characters)
- Descriptive alt text for images (117 of 171 images currently have none) and readable image file names
- Schema markup for Sylvia as author, each book, Heaven on Earth and blog articles
- Thank-you and download pages removed from search results and the sitemap; 301 redirects for merged pages
- Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and proper social sharing previews (replacing the QR-code share image)

### H. Security, Analytics & Compliance

- Privacy Policy, Terms of Sale and Refund Policy for digital products, aligned with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Consumer Law
- Spam protection on forms and two-factor login for admin accounts
- Analytics with tracking for book sales, donations and sign-ups

### I. Testing, Launch, Training & Support

- Payment, download, form, mobile and accessibility testing before launch
- Recorded video training on adding books, posting blogs and managing orders and Dream Partners
- Short written user guide
- Backups before each major step and on launch day: full content export, media library, subscriber list and Stripe records, stored privately in your name
- Your current website stays live and unchanged until the new website, subscribers, blog emails, payments and downloads have been tested and you have approved them
- Handover instructions include the backup and restoration plan: how to take regular backups after launch, where they are kept, and how the site is restored if needed
- 3 months of free support, bug fixes and minor updates after launch
