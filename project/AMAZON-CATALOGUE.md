### All Shall Be Well (verbatim)
> England, 1349. The Black Death sweeps across the land, devouring cities and villages, leaving fear and despair in its wake. Faith crumbles. Families shatter. Hope feels like a foolish dream.
>
> But amidst the darkness, a few souls still believe in love—ordinary people making small, stubborn choices to care for one another, even when it costs everything.
>
> This is Thomas Aelfric's story—a scarred boy who has lost everyone he loves. And it is Juliana's story—a woman walled into stone, inspired by the real Julian of Norwich, who whispers of a love stronger than death itself.
>
> For anyone who has ever looked at suffering and asked, "Where is God?" this moving tale of historical fiction offers a timeless answer: sometimes the greatest miracles are small acts of love that refuse to give up.
>
> If you loved Ken Follett's historical dramas or the inspirational courage of books like The Book of Longings, you will be swept away by this heart-stirring story of faith, redemption, and hope.

### How to Write and Publish a Spirit-Led Christian Book in 7 Days (verbatim; list items as on Amazon)
> There are many believers who feel something stirring in their hearts.
>
> A message. A testimony. A truth God has revealed through their walk with Him.
>
> Yet often, they don't know where to begin.
>
> Some feel unqualified. Some feel unsure. Others feel overwhelmed by the idea of writing a book at all.
>
> So, the message remains unwritten.
>
> This gentle, faith-filled guide is an invitation to change that.
>
> In How to Write and Publish a Spirit-Led Christian Book in 7 Days, Sylvia Suhr shares a simple, step-by-step approach to help you move from uncertainty to clarity — and from a blank page to a finished, published book.
>
> This is not about striving or trying to impress.
>
> It is about learning to write with God — simply, peacefully, and with purpose.
>
> Inside this book, you will discover:
> - How to recognise the message God has placed in your heart
> - How to overcome fear and begin writing with confidence
> - How to create a simple structure that makes writing easy
> - How to stay in flow without overthinking or over-editing
> - How to refine your message without losing your voice
> - How to format and publish your book in a clear, simple way
> - How to release your work with peace, not pressure
>
> You don't need to be a trained writer. You don't need perfect words. You don't need to have everything figured out.
>
> You only need a willing heart.
>
> There are people who may never hear your voice in person… but they may read your words.
>
> Your book can reach places you cannot go. It can bring encouragement, clarity, and hope — exactly when it is needed.
>
> If you sense that God is calling you to write, this book will help you take the first step — and walk it through to completion. Begin your journey today. Write with Him. Trust Him. And share what He has placed in your heart.

### 7 Days from Fear to Freedom (verbatim)
> In a world filled with uncertainty and anxiety, many people long for peace but struggle to find it.
>
> 7 Days from Fear to Freedom is a gentle devotional designed to help you turn your heart back to God and rediscover the peace that comes from trusting Him.
>
> Through seven short daily readings, Sylvia Suhr offers encouraging reflections, Scripture, and simple prayers that help guide your thoughts from fear toward faith.
>
> Each day invites you to pause, reflect on God's Word, and remember that His presence is near even in the middle of life's challenges.
>
> In this devotional you will discover:
> - How to refocus your heart on God when fear rises
> - How Scripture brings peace in uncertain times
> - How prayer helps quiet anxious thoughts
> - How trusting God leads to lasting freedom
>
> Whether you are facing a difficult season or simply longing for deeper peace, these daily reflections will encourage you to rest more fully in God's love and promises.
>
> Take a few quiet moments each day and allow God's Word to guide your heart from fear to faith. "You will keep him in perfect peace whose mind is stayed on You." — Isaiah 26:3
