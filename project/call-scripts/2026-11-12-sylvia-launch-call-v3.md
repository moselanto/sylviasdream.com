# Call with Sylvia: launch call script (v3)

Read this aloud on the call. Notes in [brackets] are for you. It runs about 40 minutes. Part 8 has prepared answers. Before the call: Moses has seen this script, you are signed in to sylviasdream.com as admin, the Stripe extension for WooCommerce is ready to connect, and the two protected ebook files are at hand. Never mention the repo or GitHub to Sylvia. Do not ask for, or look at, any password.

## What you need from her (your checklist)
1. Connect her own Stripe account to the shop (she signs in herself).
2. One real ebook purchase with her own card, a download, then a refund.
3. A test gift, if she is happy to (the agreed condition for the balance).
4. Three price answers: All Shall Be Well, SENT, and the "Freedom From Fear" name.
5. Manifesting Miracles Now: keep as Coming soon, or hide it.
6. OK to hide the two old home pages, and what to do with old payment links after 30 days.
7. Past ebook buyers: name, email, book (she exports, or she sends the email herself).
8. A look at the site on her own phone.
9. Her approval of the site, then the USD 168 balance and a training date.

## Part 1: Welcome
[Smile. Wait until she is settled. If she wishes to open in prayer, follow her lead.]

Hello Sylvia. Thank you for your time this evening. I have good news first. Your new website is live on sylviasdream.com.

Today I would like to do four things with you. Show you the live site. Connect your Stripe account to the new shop, so people can buy and download your books there. Do one real test purchase together. And answer a few small questions. It should take about 40 minutes. Is that alright?

[Pause for her answer]

## Part 2: The live site
[Share screen. Open sylviasdream.com signed out.]

This is your home page, live. The three books at the top are From Fear to Freedom, One Life and SENT.

[Open Books in the menu]

This is the Books menu. Readers can browse by topic and by language.

[Open Heaven on Earth]

This is Heaven on Earth, with the registration line you approved, the Dream Partner amounts, the GoFundMe, the videos and the photo slideshow.

[Open Going to Heaven]

And here is the Meet Jesus button you asked for.

Your Dream Partner links, your GoFundMe and your current Stripe buy buttons all still work, exactly as before. I tested each one, without paying.

Could you open sylviasdream.com on your phone now, and tell me if the text is comfortable to read?

[Pause. Listen. Note anything she wants changed.]

## Part 3: Connecting Stripe
Right now, books still sell through your existing Stripe buttons. The new shop, with the cart and the secure downloads, opens once your Stripe account is connected. Only you can connect it, because it is your account. I will guide you, and you sign in yourself. I never see your password.

[Ask her to share her screen, or guide her by voice.]

Please sign in to your website on your computer. Go to WooCommerce, then Settings, then Payments.

[Wait]

Next to Stripe, click Connect, or Set up.

[Wait]

Stripe will ask you to sign in to your Stripe account. Please sign in, the way you normally do.

[Wait. If Stripe asks for a code on her phone, give her time.]

Stripe will ask you to allow the connection. Please click Allow, or Connect.

[Wait until it returns to the website and shows Stripe as connected. Check that it is not in test mode.]

That is it. Your Stripe account is now connected to the shop. Your Dream Partner subscriptions are not touched. They carry on as before.

[You, after the call or while she waits: switch on cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay; publish Cart and Checkout; attach the two protected ebooks, When the Word Becomes Life and Guard Your Heart Renew Your Mind 6x9.]

## Part 4: One real test purchase
Now the most important check. I would like you to buy one ebook, as a reader would, with your own card. Then we refund it straight away.

[Choose a low-priced ebook. Guide her: open the book, Add to cart, Checkout, enter her details and card, Place order.]

Please check your email. You should have an order email with a download link.

[Pause]

Please click the download link. Does the book open?

[Pause. If yes:]

Wonderful. Now I refund it.

[Refund the order in WooCommerce, Orders. Tell her:]

The refund is done. It goes back to your card in a few days. Stripe may keep its small fee.

If you are happy to, the same for a gift: give five dollars once as a Dream Partner, and we refund it in Stripe. That checks the giving side end to end.

[Pause for her answer. Do not insist.]

## Part 5: A few small questions
I have a few questions about prices. I have not changed anything in Stripe.

All Shall Be Well shows 7 dollars 56 on the website, but your Stripe button charges 5 dollars 99. Which price is right?

[Pause. Write it down.]

You told me SENT is a free book. The website lists it as free, but the old SENT page still has a paid button for 5 dollars 99. Should I remove the paid button?

[Pause]

In Stripe, From Fear to Freedom is called Freedom From Fear. Buyers see that name at checkout. Would you like to rename it in Stripe? I can show you where.

[Pause]

Manifesting Miracles Now. Last time you said leave it. Do you mean keep it as Coming soon, or take it off the website for now?

[Pause]

Your two old home pages are still online in the background. Visitors who use the old addresses are already sent to the new home page. May I hide the old pages?

[Pause]

As we agreed, the old download pages keep working for 30 days, and they are hidden from search. After that, may I point the old payment links to the new shop, or would you prefer to switch them off? You do not need to decide today.

[Pause]

## Part 6: Your past buyers
Your past ebook buyers will get new secure download links. For that I need, for ebook buyers only, their name, email and which book they bought. No donor details.

You can export it from Stripe and put it in our private Google Drive folder. Not WhatsApp. Or, if you prefer, I prepare the email and you send it from your own account, so the list never leaves you. Which would you prefer?

[Pause. Note her choice and a day.]

## Part 7: Approval, training and close
Let me sum up. The site is live. Your Stripe is connected. The test purchase and download worked, and it was refunded.

[Only if that is true:] Are you happy to approve the website?

[Pause for her answer]

Thank you. As agreed, the balance of USD 168 is due now that you have approved. PayPal is usually easiest. The reference is PMF-QT-2026-0106.

Next is your training. I will show you how to add a book, post a blog, and manage orders and Dream Partners. It is recorded, so you can watch it again, and you also get the written guide. Which day suits you?

[Pause. Note the day.]

Here is what I need from you, and by when.

[Read slowly. Ask her to name a day for each.]

Your answers on the prices and on Manifesting Miracles Now, if not given today. The past buyers list, or your choice to send the email yourself. The balance. And a day for training.

Is there anything you would like to ask?

[Pause]

Thank you, Sylvia. I will send you a short summary after the call. God bless.

## Part 8: If Sylvia asks...
[Answer only what she asks. Pause after each answer.]

**Q: Is my site safe? What if something goes wrong?**
A full backup was taken just before launch. If anything went badly wrong, I can put the old site back. You still have 3 months of support.

**Q: Why do I need to connect Stripe myself?**
It is your account and your money. Only you can approve the connection. I never see your password.

**Q: Will my Dream Partners be affected?**
No. Their monthly gifts carry on exactly as before. I did not touch them.

**Q: Will my current buy buttons stop working?**
No. They keep working. Once the cart is open, we decide together whether to keep them or point them to the new shop.

**Q: Why do I pay for a test book?**
It is the only way to be sure a real reader can pay and download. We refund it straight away. Stripe may keep its small fee.

**Q: Do I pay anything more to WordPress.com?**
Only your normal plan renewal. [If she asks about fees on sales, say: Stripe charges its normal fee per sale, as it does today. If unsure of anything else, use the line below.]

**Q: When is the balance due?**
After you approve the site: a successful test purchase, a secure download, and a test gift.

**Q: Can I change things myself later?**
Yes. The training and the written guide show you how.

**If I don't know the answer**
[Say this calmly. Do not guess.]
That's a good question. I don't want to guess, so let me check and come back to you in writing by tomorrow.
